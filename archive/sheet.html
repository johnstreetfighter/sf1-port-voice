The announcer: listening sheet

EH9 (#225). Each button plays the ROM's own FIGHT! and then the take, both through the cabinet's MSM5205 at 4 kHz. Everything here is encoded ADPCM: what the player hears.

The voice

Voice: jSpNnJd6IV3EN0dVoIXm (round 1, preview 2 (archive/design/round1_preview2.wav))

Designed from: A ringside announcer at a 1980s world fighting tournament. Adult man, strong mid-range voice, a little gravel, loud and bright. He barks single words and short phrases with hard consonants, as if he's calling the action over a crowd through a cheap PA. No breathiness, no long vowels, no commentary.
